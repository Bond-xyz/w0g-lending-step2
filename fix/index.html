W0G lending fix: timelock signatures

0G mainnet · two signings from the 0G finance wallet: schedule, then execute 4 hours later · prepared Oct 14, 2026

What this fixes

One W0G setting from the Oct 12 signing went in wrong. The interest-rate target ("best lending ratio") was set to 1, which means 0.01% utilization, instead of 8000 (80%). At 1, any W0G borrow would pay tens of thousands of percent a year. This transaction sets it to 8000, the 80% kink in the W0G/a0G market plan. It also raises W0G's loan-to-value inside the 0G group (same-group borrowing) from 60% to 80%, as in the original config. Every other W0G setting stays exactly as it is.

Nothing is at risk today: nobody has supplied or borrowed W0G yet (both W0G market tokens show a supply of 0). The live status below re-checks this whenever the page is opened.

W0G borrow rate after the fix, measured on a copy of 0G mainnet: 2.6% a year at 30% utilization, 5.7% at 65%, 7.0% at 80%, 16.6% at 90%. That's in line with WETH and cbBTC on this market.

Who signs

Both steps are sent from the 0G finance wallet 0x76cB4faf35708deBADcB8eAcFFc4c050bf2C281E. It is the only address with the proposer and executor roles on the timelock, so nobody else can run either step. It is an MPC custody wallet, not a Safe: Elaine starts each request and the wallet's approvers co-sign in the custody app, the same way as the Oct 6 and Oct 12 signings.

  1. Step 1, schedule: any time. It starts a 4-hour timer (the timelock's minimum delay, 14,400 seconds).
  2. Step 2, execute: 4 hours or more after Step 1 is confirmed. This is the moment the settings change.

What the approvers see

A contract call to the timelock 0x005d73B1B62775cE76E86d00162f29Ce8145a53d with value 0. If the app decodes it, it shows schedule with the fields above. If it shows raw data, check four things:

  • It starts with 0x01d5062a (schedule).
  • It contains the lending manager address 9579cd27c44e87924735e8345c6c091a72d0b21e.
  • It contains the setting call 456eba87 followed by the W0G address 1cd0690ff9a693f5ef2dd976660a8dafc81a109c.
  • The salt ends in …0004 and the length is 548 bytes.

Check it on-chain

  • The transaction shows Success and emits CallScheduled with id 0x9dfdc24fb695be554fa7740af103230de19d6f89883fb168db71a3d95e8e484a.
  • On the timelock's Read Contract tab, isOperationPending(id) returns true.
  • getTimestamp(id) returns the unix time Step 2 opens: Step 1's block time plus 14,400. The status panel above shows it in local time.

  • Ran the whole flow on a local copy of 0G mainnet (fork at block 47,077,553): schedule 0xa57f2b9d…7b3d, then 4 hours of chain time, then execute 0x663062ed…2dec. Rate target went 1 → 8000 and 0G-group LTV 6000 → 8000, with nothing else changed.
